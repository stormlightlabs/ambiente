# Ambient Music Research

## Core Characteristics of Ambient Music

### Texture and Atmosphere

- Primary focus on texture, tone, mood, and atmosphere over traditional melody/rhythm[^1]
- Layers work synchronously to create homogenized soundscapes[^2]
- Emphasis on timbral development and spatial characteristics
- Extended duration with tracks lasting 30+ minutes
- Atmosphere prioritized above all else[^3]

### Tempo Characteristics

- Pure ambient often lacks detectable tempo[^4]
- When rhythmic elements present: mid-high 80s BPM for hybrid styles
- Musical parts grow and decay according to timbral development
- Space filled with reverb, new textures, or sonic emptiness

### Harmonic Structure

- Does not cycle through chords like pop/jazz/classical[^5]
- Luxuriates on simple chords or single notes
- Consonant harmony with repetitive patterns[^6]
- Steady drones and minimal harmonic movement
- Focus on atmospheric building over progression

### Melodic Approach

- Not melody-driven but mood and soundscape focused[^7]
- No formal melodies or steady rhythms
- Crafted from waves of aural textures
- Improvisation limited by lack of distinct melodies/chord changes

## Brian Eno's Generative Music Parameters

### Historical Tape Loop Systems

- Specific timing parameters with incommensurable cycles[^8]
- Example from "2/1": loops of 23.5s, 25.875s, 29.9375s[^9]
- Cycles wouldn't synchronize for ~27 days
- Designed for continuous variation without repetition

### Software Implementation

- SSEYO Koan software parameters (1996)[^10]
- ~200 sound sources for selection[^11]
- Automatic composition within predetermined parameters
- Criteria: must change continuously, never repeat exactly, last forever[^12]

### Modern Generative Criteria

- Must accommodate multiple listening attention levels[^13]
- Should be "as ignorable as it is interesting"[^14]
- Endless supply of never-before-heard music
- Self-sustaining algorithmic processes

## Production Techniques

### Reverb Applications

- Huge reverb with dry-leaning mix for spatial context[^15]
- Freeze function reverbs for constant drones[^16]
- Alternative reverbs (Blackhole, SuperMassive) for massive textures[^17]
- Modulated reverb tails with LFO pitch/time variation[^18]

### Delay Techniques

- Longer delay times with high feedback for atmospheric textures[^19]
- Digital delays functioning as loopers[^20]
- Comprehensive delay types with filtering and modulation[^21]
- Creative feedback loops for constant sound patterns

### Modulation Parameters

- Modulation rate: ~0.1 Hz
- Shift amount: 50-100 Hz for subtle movement
- Chorusing/flanging effects through modulation[^22]
- LFO automation for breathing, swelling effects[^26]

### Effect Chaining

- Delay before reverb: spacious, ambient effect[^27]
- Echoes fade into reverberant space
- Layered, complex ambient soundscapes
- Mystery and distance enhancement

### Synthesis Approaches

- Blendwave Modulation Synthesis[^28]
- 6 unique structures with 32 WAVEs
- Complex harmonic transitions
- Deep drones, lush pads, transparent textures

## Recommended Parameters for Presets

### Tempo Ranges

- Pure ambient: No detectable tempo
- Hybrid ambient: 80-90 BPM
- Ambient house: ≤120 BPM
- Drone/minimal: 40-60 BPM

### Reverb Settings

- Wet: 0.6-0.9 (high wetness)
- Decay: 8-20 seconds (long tails)
- Pre-delay: 0.1-0.3 seconds
- Freeze capabilities for drones

### Delay Parameters

- Time: 4n to 8n note values
- Feedback: 0.3-0.6 (moderate to high)
- Wet: 0.2-0.4 (subtle to moderate)
- Modulation for movement

### Filter Characteristics

- Type: Lowpass (most common)
- Frequency: 300-1200 Hz
- Q: 0.4-2.0 (moderate resonance)
- Modulation for breathing effects

### Instrument Volumes

- Ambient Pad: 0.3-0.5 (foundation layer)
- Granular: 0.1-0.3 (texture layer)
- Melodic: 0.05-0.4 (sparse melodic elements)
- Harmonic Drone: 0.15-0.6 (harmonic foundation)
- Rhythmic Pulse: 0.05-0.4 (when present)

### Scale Recommendations

- Pentatonic scales for simplicity
- Modal scales (Dorian, Aeolian, Lydian)
- Limited note sets (3-7 notes)
- Consonant intervals preferred

### Generative Pattern Types

- Random-walk: Gradual evolution
- Markov chains: Context-aware progression
- Static-drone: Minimal movement
- Euclidean: Rhythmic patterns when needed
